# Memento Explore Topics — Starter Questions

These are the initial curated starter questions for Explore.

They are intentionally broad, low-assumption, and easy to answer. They should help Memento build enough context to later generate more personalized questions.

---

## Growing Up
`growing-up`

1. What is one of the earliest places you remember clearly?
2. What were you like as a child?
3. What did you enjoy doing when you were younger?
4. What was school like for you growing up?
5. Was there something you were especially curious about as a child?
6. What is a small childhood memory that still makes you smile?
7. Who influenced you most while you were growing up?
8. What did a normal day look like for you when you were younger?
9. What is something from your childhood you still enjoy today?
10. In what ways do you think you have changed since you were younger?

---

## People I Love
`people-i-love`

1. Who are some of the people you feel closest to?
2. What do you value most in a friendship?
3. Who can usually make you laugh?
4. What makes you feel cared for by another person?
5. Is there someone you enjoy spending time with even when you are doing nothing special?
6. What qualities make you trust someone?
7. Who has taught you something important simply by being in your life?
8. How do you usually show people that you care about them?
9. What kind of people do you naturally feel comfortable around?
10. What is something you appreciate about the people currently in your life?

---

## The Life I Live
`the-life-i-live`

1. What does a normal weekday look like for you?
2. What part of your day do you usually enjoy most?
3. What is one routine you would miss if you stopped doing it?
4. What does home feel like to you?
5. How do you usually spend your free time?
6. Are you more of a morning person or a night person?
7. What small thing makes an ordinary day better for you?
8. What is something you do almost every day without thinking about it?
9. Where do you spend most of your time these days?
10. What would your ideal ordinary day look like?

## Things I Love
`things-i-love`

1. What is something you can talk about for a long time without getting bored?
2. What kind of music do you enjoy most?
3. What is a food you almost never get tired of?
4. What do you like doing when you have time completely to yourself?
5. Is there a place you always enjoy being?
6. What kind of movies, shows, books, or stories do you usually enjoy?
7. What is one hobby or interest you would happily spend more time on?
8. What is something small that brings you a surprising amount of joy?
9. What is something you have been really into lately?
10. If you had an entirely free day, what would you probably choose to do?

---

## Who I Am
`who-i-am`

1. How would you describe yourself to someone who had never met you?
2. What is something people often notice about you?
3. What part of your personality do you like most?
4. What kind of situations bring out the best in you?
5. What is something you have become more confident about over time?
6. Do you usually prefer planning ahead or figuring things out as you go?
7. What is something you care about more deeply than people might expect?
8. What is one thing you wish people understood about you more easily?
9. When do you feel most like yourself?
10. What is something about yourself that has changed in the last few years?

## Work & Purpose
`work-purpose`

1. What kind of work do you enjoy doing?
2. What makes work feel meaningful to you?
3. What are you naturally good at?
4. What is something you would like to become better at?
5. What motivates you to work toward something difficult?
6. What does success mean to you personally?
7. What kind of problem do you enjoy solving?
8. What would you like your work to give you besides money?
9. Is there something you would like to build, create, or contribute to?
10. If you could spend more time on one kind of work, what would it be?

## Beliefs & Faith
`beliefs-faith`

1. What values are most important to the way you try to live?
2. Is there a belief or principle that guides many of your decisions?
3. What helps you decide what is right or wrong for you?
4. What gives your life a sense of meaning?
5. Are there traditions, practices, or rituals that are important to you?
6. What is something you believe strongly about how people should treat one another?
7. Has the way you see the world changed as you have gotten older?
8. Are there questions about life you find yourself thinking about often?
9. What helps you feel grounded when life feels uncertain?
10. Is there a belief, teaching, or idea you often return to?

## Where I Come From
`where-i-come-from`

1. Where do you think of when you think about where you come from?
2. What is something about your culture or background that you appreciate?
3. Are there foods that strongly remind you of home or family?
4. What traditions did you grow up around?
5. Is there a saying, expression, or habit from your family that has stayed with you?
6. What place has had the biggest influence on the person you became?
7. What is something about your upbringing that you would like to preserve?
8. Are there stories about your family that you heard often while growing up?
9. What parts of your background do you feel most connected to today?
10. What is something about where you come from that you would want someone else to understand?

## Stories & Adventures
`stories-adventures`

1. What is a day you remember as being especially fun?
2. Is there a place you have visited that stayed with you?
3. What is one experience you would happily do again?
4. What is a funny moment you still enjoy telling people about?
5. Have you ever tried something simply because you were curious?
6. What is one of the most memorable things you have done recently?
7. Is there somewhere you would love to visit someday?
8. What kind of experiences make you feel most alive?
9. What is an ordinary outing or trip that became unexpectedly memorable?
10. If you could relive one enjoyable day from your life, which kind of day would you choose?

## What Life Taught Me
`what-life-taught-me`

1. What is something you understand better now than you did a few years ago?
2. What is a lesson you learned through experience rather than advice?
3. What is something you used to worry about that matters less to you now?
4. What advice has stayed with you over the years?
5. What is something you have learned about yourself recently?
6. Has there been a mistake that taught you something useful?
7. What helps you keep going when something is difficult?
8. What is one opinion you have changed your mind about?
9. What is something you wish you had understood earlier in life?
10. What lesson would you want your younger self to know?

## What's Ahead
`whats-ahead`

1. What are you looking forward to right now?
2. What is something you would like to experience in the next few years?
3. Is there something you have always wanted to learn?
4. What would you like more of in your life?
5. What kind of person do you hope to become?
6. Is there a place you hope to visit someday?
7. What is something you would like to create or accomplish?
8. What would make the next chapter of your life feel meaningful?
9. Is there something you keep telling yourself you will do someday?
10. When you imagine a good future for yourself, what does it look like?

---

## Review Notes

When reviewing these questions, check each one against four criteria:

1. **Low assumption** — it should not presume a specific life experience.
2. **Easy entry** — the user should be able to answer without needing a long period of reflection.
3. **Useful context** — the answer should teach Memento something meaningful.
4. **Room to deepen** — the answer should create possible directions for future personalized questions.
